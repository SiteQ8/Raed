# {ORG_NAME} Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Last updated: {DATE}

## Scope

This policy covers vulnerabilities in {SCOPE}. {ORG_NAME} receives reports about them, coordinates their remediation, assigns CVE IDs and publishes CVE Records.

## How to report

Email {SECURITY_EMAIL} in Arabic or English, optionally encrypted with the key at {PGP_URL}. Please include the affected product and version, the type and location of the weakness, steps to reproduce and any proof of concept, the impact of exploitation, and the name you want credited (or that you prefer to stay anonymous).

## What we commit to

- Acknowledge your report within three business days.
- Assess it and tell you our decision within ten business days, explaining why if we conclude it is not a vulnerability.
- Reserve a CVE ID once confirmed, coordinate a fix date with the supplier and keep you informed.
- Publish the advisory and record when a fix is available or 90 days after the report, whichever comes first. We may publish earlier if the vulnerability is exploited in the wild, or later by agreement with you.
- Publish the CVE Record within 24 hours of publicly disclosing the ID, and never later than 72 hours (CNA Rules 4.5.1.3 and 4.5.1.4).
- Credit you in the advisory and the record unless you ask otherwise.

## Good faith research

We will not pursue anyone who researches in good faith under this policy: avoid service disruption, do not access or keep other people's data, stop once the vulnerability is demonstrated, and give us reasonable time before disclosure.

## Out of scope

Denial of service, social engineering, physical testing, and products of other organisations. Reports about products in another CNA's scope are passed to that CNA with your consent.

## CVE assignment

{ORG_NAME} is a CVE Numbering Authority. Our scope is listed on cve.org and we follow the CNA Operational Rules in force.
